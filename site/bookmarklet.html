‹ ryōgae 両替

Prices on websites

A bookmark that converts every price on the page you're viewing (₩, 원, 만원, $, €, ¥, ₪, S$) into your home currency. Tap a tag to see all currencies. Run it again to remove the tags.

≈ ryōgae
Chrome on a computer
  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the orange ≈ ryōgae button onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. On any shop, click the bookmark.
Chrome on Android
  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet code.
  2. Bookmark this page (⋮ → ☆), then edit that bookmark: name it ryogae and replace its URL with the copied code.
  3. On a shop, tap the address bar, type ryogae and tap the bookmark that appears (with the star icon).
Safari on iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet code.
  2. Share → Add Bookmark (name it ryogae) and save.
  3. Bookmarks → Edit → tap the new bookmark and replace its address with the copied code.
  4. On a shop, open Bookmarks and tap ryogae.

Your home currency and the latest rates are built into the bookmark. Each run it also tries to fetch fresh rates; if the site blocks that, the built-in ones are used. Changed your home currency? Come back here and re-create the bookmark.
